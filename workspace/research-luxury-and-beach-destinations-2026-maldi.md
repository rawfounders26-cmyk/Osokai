The Maldives The Maldives remains our number-one recommendation for couples seeking complete seclusion. The overwater-villa concept has been copied everywhere from Bora Bora to the Maldives’ own neighbours in the Indian Ocean, but nowhere else delivers the combination of reef access, butler service, and sheer silence. The North Malé Atoll is among the most accessible luxury bases in the country, and it shows why this destination endures. The water clarity is staggering — reef sharks are often visible from villa decks without polarised lenses. Transfers remain the biggest logistical headache: seaplane schedules are weather-dependent and delays of two to three hours are common, so always build in a buffer day.
